In this Beacon, you'll build a real, working mini app — using nothing but words.

No code. No technical skills. Just a clear idea and a prompt.

You'll use a tool called Lovable, which turns your description into a fully functioning web app — one that you can actually open in a browser and share with anyone.

This is one of the most surprising things AI can do today. Let's try it.

🛠️Meet the
tool

Lovable is an AI-powered app builder. You describe what you want, and it writes all the code for you — then instantly publishes your app online.

You don't need to know anything about programming. You just chat with it, like you would with Claude or ChatGPT.

🔗 Go here to get started: https://lovable.dev

Sign up for free — no credit card needed.

A few things to know about the free plan:

  • You get 5 credits per day (about 30 per month)
  • Each message you send uses roughly 1 credit
  • Your app will be published at a public link (a lovable.app address)
  • That's more than enough for this Beacon
🧪Try it
now

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

You're going to build a simple daily habit tracker — an app where you can tick off small habits each day, like drinking water, going for a walk, or reading for 10 minutes.

Step 1 — Sign in and start a new project

Go to https://lovable.dev and sign up (or log in if you already have an account).

On the main screen, you'll see a text box where you can describe your app. That's all you need.

Step 2 — Type this prompt and press Enter:

Build a simple daily habit tracker. It should show a list of habits I want to do each day — like drinking water, going for a walk, and reading for 10 minutes. I should be able to tick each one off, and the page should show how many I've completed today. Keep the design clean and friendly.

Now sit back and watch. Lovable will write the code and build your app — usually in under a minute.

Step 3 — Open your app

When it's done, your app is already running in the right-hand preview panel. Try ticking off a habit — it works!

To get a shareable link, click the blue share icon in the top right corner. A Publish panel will open. Work through the steps:

  1. Your website URL is already set — click Continue
  2. Leave visibility on Public — click Continue
  3. Leave the website info blank — click Continue
  4. Click Publish

You'll see "Your app is live!" — with a real link like good-deeds-today.lovable.app. Click View app to open it in a browser, or Share to send the link to anyone.

Step 4 — Make one small change (optional)

If you'd like to tweak something, just type a follow-up message — for example:

Change the colour scheme to something calm and green.

Or:

Add a fourth habit: "No phone before breakfast."

Lovable will update the app instantly.

👀What to
notice

Look at the app running in the preview panel — or open it in a browser tab.

Notice that the checkboxes actually work. When you tick a habit, the counter updates. This isn't just a picture of an app — it's a real, interactive web app, built entirely from your description.

The bigger insight: you just built software by writing a sentence. That's what "no-code AI" means — and it changes what's possible for anyone, regardless of technical background.

💡Good to
know

About the free plan credits:

Each message you send uses roughly one credit. You have 5 per day — so plan your prompts thoughtfully. One well-written prompt is usually better than five quick ones.

Your app has a real link:

Lovable publishes your app online automatically. The address will look something like yourproject.lovable.app. You can share it with anyone — they can open it in a browser, no download needed.

You can always keep editing:

Come back tomorrow with fresh credits and keep refining. You might add more habits, change the colours, or try a completely different app idea.

Some ideas if you want to try another app:

  • A simple recipe card with ingredients and steps
  • A "things I'm grateful for" journal
  • A birthday reminder list
  • A packing list for travel
  • A reading list with checkboxes
  • A quiz with 3 questions about your favourite topic
  • A countdown timer to a date you're looking forward to

Each one is just a prompt away.
