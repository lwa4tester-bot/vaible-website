🎯 What you'll learn

In this Beacon, you'll discover how to turn any piece of text into a real, natural-sounding voice — no microphone, no recording, no editing skills needed.

You'll use ElevenLabs, one of the most impressive voice AI tools available today. By the end, you'll have listened to your own words spoken back to you in a voice you chose.

🛠️ Meet the tool

ElevenLabs → https://elevenlabs.io

ElevenLabs turns written text into spoken audio — and the result sounds remarkably human.

It's used by podcasters, educators, and content creators to produce voiceovers, narrations, and audiobooks. But you don't need any of that background. You can use it just to hear how something sounds, or to create a short clip for a practical or personal use.

The free plan gives you enough credits each month to experiment comfortably.

🔊 What is text-to-speech?

Text-to-speech is exactly what it sounds like: you type something, and the AI reads it out loud.

But modern text-to-speech is nothing like the robotic voices of the past. ElevenLabs produces voices that pause naturally, shift tone, and even sound emotional — depending on what they're saying.

You choose the voice. You provide the text. ElevenLabs does the rest.

🌍 More than one language

One of the most useful things about ElevenLabs is that it handles multiple languages naturally.

Type in English, French, Spanish, German — or mix them — and the voice will follow along without missing a beat. This makes it useful for:

  • Listening to a text in a language you're learning
  • Creating a narration for an audience in another country
  • Simply hearing how your own writing sounds when read aloud
🧪 Try it now
1
Go to 👉 https://elevenlabs.io and sign up for a free account. Your Gmail works perfectly here.

You'll go through a short setup — answer what feels right, and click Skip whenever you see the option. It takes about a minute.

Once you're in, click Text to Speech in the left sidebar.

2
Pick any voice from the list, paste the text below into the text box, and press Generate:
Ready-made prompt
Good morning. Today is a great day to try something new. You've already come so far — and this is just the beginning.

Then generate the same text with two or three different voices. Notice how the same words feel completely different depending on who is "speaking" them.

Want to try your own text? Here are some ideas:

  1. A short introduction of yourself
  2. A birthday message for a friend
  3. A motivational quote you love
  4. A recipe intro — "Welcome to today's recipe..."
  5. A fake movie trailer narration
  6. A short travel guide for your hometown
  7. A calming bedtime story opening line
  8. A news-style headline read aloud

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

👀 What to notice

After your first clip plays, listen carefully to the pauses.

The voice doesn't read at a flat, even pace — it breathes, it pauses at commas, it softens slightly at the end of a sentence. It sounds like someone who means what they're saying.

The aha moment: this isn't a robot reading text. It's AI that has learned how humans actually speak — and once you hear that difference, you can't unhear it.

💡 Good to know

The free plan gives you a limited number of characters per month — enough for several minutes of audio, which is plenty for exploring.

No downloads required. Everything runs in your browser.

If you spot features like Voice Lab or Voice Cloning, feel free to ignore them for now. Those are for a later adventure. Today is just about listening.
