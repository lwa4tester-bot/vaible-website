🎯 What you'll learn

By the end of this Beacon, you'll know when to trust AI — and when to double-check. You'll also learn how to use Perplexity and your favorite chatbot to verify information quickly.

⚠️ AI can be wrong

What is a hallucination?

AI tools can state something incorrectly — and sound completely confident while doing it. This is called a hallucination. The AI doesn't know it's wrong. It's not lying — it just fills in gaps with plausible-sounding information.

AI tends to struggle most with: recent events, specific numbers and statistics, quotes and sources, and local or niche information.

🛡️ How to fact-check

Two ways to verify

With Perplexity: Take any surprising claim and ask: "Is it true that [claim]? Please show me your sources."

With any AI tool: Ask: "How confident are you in this answer?" or "Are there any parts of this you're not sure about?" Many AI tools will flag their own uncertainty when you ask directly.

🧪 Try it now

Get a fact — then check it

Step 1 — Get a claim

Ready-made prompt
"Tell me 3 interesting facts about the human body. Keep each one to one sentence."

Pick one that surprises you — or one you're not sure about.

Step 2 — Check it with Perplexity

Fact-check prompt
"Is it true that [paste the fact here]? Please show me your sources."
👀 What to notice

Did Perplexity agree?

Look at what Perplexity says about the fact you picked. Did it confirm it, contradict it, or offer a more nuanced version?

Notice that the AI's original answer might have been almost right — correct in general, but off on a specific detail. That's the gap worth watching: AI often gets the big picture right but slips on the fine print.

💡 Good to know

A simple rule

Think of AI like a very well-read friend. They know a lot. They're helpful and quick. But they're not always right. For anything that affects your health, finances, legal situation, or that you plan to share publicly — always verify with a trusted source.
