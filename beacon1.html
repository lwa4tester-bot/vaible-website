🌍What is AI

In one sentence

AI is a tool you talk to like a person. It helps you write, think, plan, learn, and create.

Not a robot. Not magic. Not dangerous.

A very smart assistant that never sleeps, never loses patience, and never judges you.

Three voices, one question

Not just different words — different personalities. One might be warmer, one more precise, one more playful.

That difference? It matters. And by the end of this course, you'll know exactly which one to reach for.

Everyone gets different answers — and that's completely normal. AI responses vary each time. That's not a bug, that's how it works.
