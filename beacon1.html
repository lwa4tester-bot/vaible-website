🎯 What you'll learn

In this Beacon, you'll meet the three AI tools you'll use throughout this course — and have your very first conversation with each one.

🌍 What is AI — in one sentence

AI is a tool you talk to like a person. It helps you write, think, plan, learn, and create.

Not a robot. Not magic. Not dangerous.

A very smart assistant that never sleeps, never loses patience, and never judges you.

🧪 Try it now

Your first prompt

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

Open all three tools and paste this prompt into each one:

Ready-made prompt
"Explain [why we dream] to me like I'm hearing about it for the first time. Keep it short and make it interesting."

Swap the topic in brackets — click one:

why the sky is blue how memory works why music gives you chills what black holes actually are why we forget things so easily how the internet actually works why cats purr what happens when we exercise why some people need more sleep

Send the same prompt to all three. Then read the answers side by side.

👀 What to notice

Three voices, one question

Not just different words — different personalities. One might be warmer, one more precise, one more playful.

That difference? It matters. And by the end of this course, you'll know exactly which one to reach for.

💡 Good to know

Different every time

Everyone gets different answers — and that's completely normal. AI responses vary each time. That's not a bug, that's how it works.
