🛠️Meet the
tool

Leonardo AI is a dedicated AI image generator — built specifically for creating visuals.

You type a description, and it produces a beautiful image in seconds. Unlike Gemini, this is all it does — and it shows.

👉 https://leonardo.ai

Click Sign in with Google — your Gmail account works instantly. No credit card needed.

🖼️ From
words to pictures

AI image tools work the same way as everything else you've done so far: you describe what you want, and the tool creates it.

This description is called a prompt — the same word you've seen in Gemini and ChatGPT.

The difference? The result is a visual, not a paragraph.

A simple sentence is enough to get something beautiful. You don't need to be an artist. You just need to put words to the image in your head.

🧪Try it
now

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

Step 1 — Generate in Gemini

Go to 👉 https://gemini.google.com

Type this prompt:

Generate an image of a cozy reading nook with a warm lamp, autumn leaves outside the window, watercolor style

Step 2 — Try the same prompt in Leonardo AI

Go to 👉 https://leonardo.ai → sign in with Google → type the same prompt in the box at the top → click Generate

Compare the two results. Same words — different style, different mood, different feel.

👀What to
notice

Look at the two images side by side.

They came from the exact same sentence — but they look completely different.

That's the real insight: every AI tool has its own visual personality. The prompt is the same; the interpretation is not.

💡Good to
know

Leonardo AI on the free plan generates public images — anyone browsing the community gallery can see them. Don't use personal photos or private information in your prompts.

Not happy with the result? Just generate again — every attempt produces something new.

How much can I generate for free?

Gemini gives you up to 100 images per day. Leonardo AI gives you 150 tokens per day — enough for roughly 25–37 images. Both reset daily.
