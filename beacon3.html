Same question, three different answers

You've already met them in Beacon 1. Now it's time to get to know them a little better.

ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini can all do similar things — but they have different styles. Like people, really. Same question, three different answers.

There's no best one. There's only the one that feels right to you.

🧪Try it

One prompt, three personalities

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

Open all three tools and paste the exact same prompt into each one:

Ready-made prompt
"I have to give a short speech at my friend's wedding next month. I've never done this before and I'm a little nervous. Can you help me get started?"

Send it to all three. Then read the answers side by side.

👀What to
notice

You're not looking for the best speech

You're looking at how each tool talks to you.

  • Does it jump straight into structure — or does it acknowledge the nerves first?
  • Does it feel warm, or efficient, or somewhere in between?
  • Is the tone something you'd actually want to read again?

One of them will probably feel more like your kind of helper. That's your companion.

💡Good to
know

You don't have to pick just one

You don't have to stick with your choice forever. Most people end up using two or three tools depending on the task. That's completely normal.

Think of it like pens. You might use a different one for shopping lists and thank-you cards.
