🎯What you'll

By the end of this Beacon, you'll know how to turn a simple idea into a beautiful, ready-to-present slide deck — in just a few minutes. No design skills needed. No PowerPoint frustration. Just a short prompt, and Gamma does the rest.

🛠️Meet the
tool

Gamma — https://gamma.app

Gamma is an AI-powered presentation tool. You describe what you want, and it builds the slides for you — with layout, text, and images already in place. The result looks professional straight away, and you can edit anything you don't like.

Gamma works in your browser. You'll need to create a free account — your Gmail address is perfect for this.

✨From idea
to slides in minutes

Most people spend hours on presentations. Choosing a template, arranging text, finding images, tweaking colours — it adds up fast.

Gamma flips that around. You start with a sentence or two describing your topic, and Gamma builds the whole thing. You review it, adjust what you want, and you're done.

It's not magic — but it's pretty close. 😊

🎨You're still
in control

Gamma makes the first version, but you decide what stays.

Before it generates, you can choose:

  • How many slides you want
  • How much text per slide (minimal, concise, detailed)
  • The visual theme — colours and style
  • The image style — illustrated, photographic, abstract

And once the presentation is ready, every slide is editable. Click on any text to change it. Don't like an image? Replace it in one click.

💾Your presentation
is saved automatically

You don't need to click "Save." Gamma saves everything to your account as you work. You can come back later, make changes, or share it with someone directly from Gamma.

When you're ready to take it elsewhere, you can export it — as a PowerPoint file, a Google Slides presentation, a PDF, or a set of images. Just click Share in the top right corner, then choose Export.

🧪Try it
now

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

Step 1 — Go to Gamma

Open https://gamma.app and sign in with your Gmail account. If it's your first time, Gamma will ask a few quick questions (personal use, team size, how you heard about them) — just pick whatever fits and move on.

Step 2 — Start a new presentation

Click New or look for the Generate option. Make sure Presentation is selected at the top.

Step 3 — Enter your prompt

In the text box, type:

A short introduction to the benefits of walking every day — 5 slides

Leave the other settings as they are (the defaults work great) and click Generate outline.

Step 4 — Review the outline

Gamma shows you the slide structure before it builds anything. Have a quick look — you can edit titles or bullet points here if you want. When you're happy, scroll down and click Generate.

Step 5 — Explore your presentation

In about 30 seconds, your slide deck is ready. Click through the slides on the left. Try clicking on a text block to edit it. See what the images look like.

Step 6 — Export it

Click Share in the top right corner, then Export. Try downloading it as a PowerPoint or PDF.

Want to try a different topic? Here are some ideas:

  • How to start a vegetable garden at home
  • 5 ways to save money on groceries
  • A beginner's guide to better sleep
  • Things to do in [your city] this weekend
  • How to plan a family trip on a budget
  • The basics of recycling at home
  • Simple stretches to do at your desk
  • How to teach kids good money habits
👀What to
notice

Look at the slide titles Gamma chose. You asked for a short introduction — notice how it created a logical flow on its own: a problem, a solution, benefits, practical tips, and a closing call to action.

The "aha" here: you gave Gamma one sentence, and it made all the editorial decisions for you. That's the real time-saver — not just the design, but the thinking.

💡Good to
know

The free plan gives you 400 credits.

Each presentation uses some credits. 400 is enough to create several decks and explore the tool comfortably — no payment needed.

Gamma said 10 slides, not 5.

You asked for 5 slides, but Gamma generated 10. That's normal — it sometimes adjusts based on the topic. You can always delete slides you don't need.

You can edit after generating.

Nothing is locked in. Click any text to change it, click any image to swap it. Think of the first version as a very good first draft.

Export goes through Share.

It's not the most obvious path — but Share → Export is where you'll find PowerPoint, Google Slides, PDF, and PNG options.

Gamma works in other languages too.

On the Generate screen, there's a language dropdown — set to English by default. You can switch it to your own language before generating, and Gamma will create the whole presentation in that language instead.
