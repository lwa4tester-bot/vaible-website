🌍A translation
superpower

Translate anything, instantly

Need to read a restaurant menu in Italian? Got an email in French and have no idea what it says? AI tools can translate almost any language — instantly, and for free.

You don't need to copy-paste into a separate app. Just ask directly in the chat. It's as simple as typing what you need:

Translate to English

"Please translate this into English: Bonjour, comment puis-je vous aider?"

Translate to another language

"How do you say 'Where is the train station?' in Japanese?"

Check the tone

"Translate this email from German, and let me know if the tone sounds formal or casual."

📚More than
translation

Use it to learn, not just to translate

AI doesn't just swap words. It understands context. You can also use AI as a gentle language tutor. Ask it to:

  • Explain a grammar rule in simple terms
  • Give you 5 useful phrases for travelling in Spain
  • Correct a sentence you wrote in French

You're in control of the pace. Ask as many follow-up questions as you like.

👀What to
notice

More than just words

A regular translation app gives you words. This gave you something more — tone, context, and an alternative version.

That's not translation. That's understanding.

💡Good to
know

Good enough for everyday life

AI translation works best with everyday language. Very technical documents — legal contracts, medical reports — are better handled by a professional translator. But for daily life, AI is more than enough.
