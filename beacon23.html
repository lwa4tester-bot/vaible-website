🧠AI as
a daily habit

You've learned a lot in this course. You can write emails, summarise documents, make images, and edit videos. But here's the thing most people miss: AI becomes truly useful when you use it regularly, not just occasionally.

Think of it like a coffee machine. Having one is nice. But the real value comes from making it part of your morning.

This Beacon is about building that habit — starting small, keeping it easy, and making AI feel like a natural part of your day.

📅The morning
briefing

One of the simplest and most powerful AI habits is the morning check-in. Instead of starting your day scattered, you open Claude or ChatGPT and ask:

"Help me plan my day. I have a team meeting at 10am, a report to finish by Friday, and I want to leave early today. What should I focus on first?"

That's it. You get a calm, clear starting point for the day — in under a minute.

You don't need to install anything. You don't need a special plan. You just need to make it a habit.

📝Adapting to
what you need

The beauty of using Claude or ChatGPT as a personal assistant is that you can change what you ask — every single day.

Some days you need to think through a problem:

"I have to give difficult feedback to a colleague. How do I approach this kindly but clearly?"

Some days you need to stay on top of tasks:

"I have 6 things on my to-do list. Help me decide what to tackle first and what can wait."

Some days you just need a second opinion:

"I wrote this message to a client. Does it sound professional?"

There's no fixed formula. The best personal assistant adapts to you — and so does AI.

🔁The weekly
reset

Another habit worth building: the end-of-week reset.

On Friday afternoon (or Sunday evening), take 5 minutes and ask:

"Here's what I got done this week: [list]. And here's what's coming up next week: [list]. Help me think about priorities and anything I might be forgetting."

This isn't about productivity pressure. It's about starting the next week with a clear head instead of a foggy one.

🧪Try it
now

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

Let's build your first AI morning routine — right now.

Step 1 — Open Claude (https://claude.ai) and paste this prompt:

"Act as my personal assistant for the day. My name is [your name]. I have these tasks today:

- Reply to an email from my kid's teacher

- Prepare a short report for tomorrow's meeting

- Reschedule a dentist appointment

- Figure out what to cook for dinner this week

I also want to make sure I take a short break at some point. Help me plan my morning."

Read what it suggests. Notice how it organises your time — does it prioritise anything? Does it suggest something you hadn't thought of?

Step 2 — Try the same thing in ChatGPT (https://chatgpt.com) using the same prompt.

Compare the two responses. Which one felt more like a real assistant to you? Which response would you actually follow?

Not sure what to put in your task list? Here are some ideas:

  • Reply to an email from your kid's school
  • Prepare for an upcoming meeting
  • Call to reschedule a doctor's appointment
  • Figure out meals for the week
  • Pay a bill or sort out some paperwork
  • Write a message you've been putting off
  • Book something for the family
  • Tidy up your inbox
  • Plan a birthday or a small event
  • Think through a decision you've been avoiding
👀What to
notice

After Step 2, look at both responses side by side.

Notice whether one of them breaks your day into time blocks, while the other gives you a simple list. Or maybe one asks a follow-up question before diving in.

The "aha" here: these are two different AI personalities. Same question, different instincts. Neither is wrong — but one might just click better with how you think.

💡Good to
know

You don't need to remember anything between sessions.

Claude and ChatGPT don't keep your history by default — each conversation starts fresh. That's actually fine for a daily routine. Think of it as a clean slate every morning.

Shorter prompts work too.

You don't always need a detailed prompt. "I have 4 meetings and a deadline today — where do I start?" is enough. The AI fills in the rest.

This is the most underrated AI habit.

Flashy tools get the attention. But the people who get the most out of AI are often the ones who use it quietly, every day, for the small stuff.
