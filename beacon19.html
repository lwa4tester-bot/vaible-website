🎙️ From
words to a mini-podcast

A podcast sounds complicated. But at its core, it's just a voice telling a story.

You already know how to do the hard part:

  • In B15, you used ElevenLabs to turn text into a natural-sounding voice.
  • In B17, you used CapCut AI to add captions and audio edits to a video.

In this Beacon, you'll combine them — write a short script, generate the voice with ElevenLabs, then bring it into CapCut to add a music background and simple polish.

No microphone. No recording studio. Just a few lines of text and two tools you've already met.

✍️ Writing
your script

The script is everything. A good podcast moment doesn't need to be long — it needs to feel like someone is talking to you, not at you.

Here's a simple format that works every time:

  1. Open with a question or a moment — something the listener can relate to
  2. Share a thought or a little story — 3–5 sentences is plenty
  3. End with one takeaway — something they'll remember

Aim for around 60–90 words. That's roughly 30–45 seconds of audio — the perfect length for a first attempt.

You can write the script yourself, or ask ChatGPT or Claude to help you draft one. Just give it a topic and say: "Write me a short, friendly 60-word podcast script about [your topic]."

🧪Try it
now

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

Step 1 — Create your voice clip in ElevenLabs

Go to https://elevenlabs.io and sign in.

Write or paste your script into the text box. Choose a voice that matches the mood — warm, calm, and conversational works best for a podcast feel. Generate the audio and download the MP3 file to your device.

Here's a ready-made script if you'd like to start with something:

"Have you ever felt like there's just too much to learn and not enough time? I used to feel that way about technology. Then I started experimenting — just five minutes a day, no pressure. Turns out, curiosity is the only skill you actually need. Everything else follows."

Step 2 — Add music and finish it in CapCut AI

Go to https://www.capcut.com and open a new project.

Import your downloaded MP3 as the main audio track. Then browse the Music library and add a soft background track — look for "podcast", "calm", or "lo-fi" in the search bar. Adjust the background music volume so the voice stays clearly in front.

Export your finished piece using the Download button.

Idea bank — pick a topic that feels natural to you:

  1. Something small you learned recently
  2. A morning habit you've kept (or tried to keep)
  3. One thing that surprised you about AI
  4. A place you've always wanted to visit
  5. Advice you'd give your younger self
  6. A book, film, or song that stayed with you
  7. One thing that made you smile this week
👀What to
notice

When you play back your finished clip, pay attention to one thing: does it sound like a real person talking?

Notice the small pauses between sentences. Notice how the background music changes the whole mood — the same voice feels completely different over calm piano versus an upbeat beat.

That's the insight: a podcast isn't really about the recording quality. It's about the atmosphere. And you just built that atmosphere entirely in software.

💡Good to
know

Free plan heads-up: ElevenLabs gives you a limited number of characters per month on the free tier — your 60–90 word script uses very little of that. CapCut's free plan is fully capable for this project.

Your voice, your rules: You can use the AI-generated voice exactly as it is — or treat it as a rough draft. Some people listen back and rewrite the script until the voice sounds just right. That back-and-forth is part of the process.

Video or no video? Your call. When you import an audio file into CapCut, the preview will be black — that's normal. You can either upload a photo or image to use as a background, or simply download the project as-is. Both work perfectly. Try it both ways and see what you prefer.
