🎯 What you'll learn

You'll discover how to make AI read long documents for you — and pull out exactly what you need. By the end, you'll know how to summarize an article, PDF, or report in seconds.

📄 The reading problem

You don't have time to read everything

Your inbox has a 12-page report. Your browser has 7 open tabs. There's a PDF from two weeks ago you still haven't touched. AI can scan a long document in seconds and tell you what matters.

🤖 Two tools, one topic

Claude + NotebookLM

Claude is excellent at reading text — paste an article or upload a PDF using the paperclip icon. Ask: "Summarize this in 5 bullet points." or "What are the main takeaways?"

NotebookLM is built specifically for working with documents. Upload multiple documents at once — it finds connections between them. → notebooklm.google.com

🧪 Try it now

Same article, two tools

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

Step 1 — With Claude

Ready-made prompt
"Please summarize this article in 5 simple bullet points. Highlight anything that might be useful in everyday life: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleep"

Step 2 — With NotebookLM

Create a new notebook, add the same link as a website source, then ask:

NotebookLM prompt
"What are the most important things I should know about sleep? Keep it practical."
👀 What to notice

15,000 words — in under a minute

Same article. Same question. Two different experiences. Claude feels like a quick conversation. NotebookLM feels like a structured assistant that stays with the document. And that Wikipedia article on sleep? It's 15,000 words long. You just got the key points in under a minute.

💡 Good to know

A few things worth knowing

File size matters. Claude handles most PDFs well, but if a document is very long or heavy, NotebookLM manages it better. AI summaries are helpful — but not perfect. For anything important, always read the relevant section yourself too.
