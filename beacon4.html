How to ask an AI to write a professional email for you — even when you're not sure what to say.

✉️Finding the
right words

The awkward email problem

Sometimes you need to send an email, but you just don't know how to start. Maybe you want to cancel a subscription. Or say no to something. Finding the right words can feel uncomfortable.

AI is great at this. You describe what you want — and it writes the email for you.

📝A real
example

Let's try it

Imagine you want to cancel a gym membership. You don't want to be rude, but you want to be clear. Here's what you could type into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini:

Example prompt
"Write a short, polite email to cancel my gym membership. I joined 3 months ago but I'm not using it anymore. Keep it friendly and professional."

That's it. No need to write anything yourself — just explain the situation in plain words.

The AI will write a complete, polished email for you. Read it, adjust anything you'd like to change, and send it. If you don't like the first result, just ask for a different version:

"Make it shorter."
"Make it more formal."
"Add that I'd like a confirmation."
🧪Try it
now

Your turn

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

Open Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini — whichever you prefer. Think of an email you've been putting off. A few ideas:

following up on something you never heard back about saying no to an invitation without sounding rude asking a colleague for a favour you feel awkward about writing a polite complaint to a shop or service thanking someone you should have thanked a while ago

Describe your situation to the AI in plain words — like you'd explain it to a friend:

Ready-made prompt
"Write a short, polite email to follow up on a job application I sent two weeks ago. I haven't heard back. I want to sound professional but not pushy."

Read the result. Then ask for one small change — just to see what happens.

👀What to
notice

The awkward part is gone

That thing you were putting off? The AI just handled the awkward part for you — in seconds. And it doesn't sound robotic. It sounds like something you'd actually send.

💡Good to
know

This works for many types of emails

Thank-you email

"Write a short thank-you email to a colleague who helped me with a project."

Complaint email

"Write a polite but firm complaint email to a hotel about a noisy room."

Just describe the situation, and let the AI do the writing.
