📧 Step one

You'll need a Gmail account

All three tools in this course — ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini — can be accessed with a free Google account. If you already have Gmail, you're all set.

If not, here's how to create one:

  1. Go to accounts.google.com/signup
  2. Enter your name and choose a Gmail address
  3. Create a password and complete the short verification
  4. That's it — your Google account is ready
💡 Two-factor authentication: Google may ask you to add a phone number for security during setup. This is optional but recommended — it protects your account and makes recovery easier.
