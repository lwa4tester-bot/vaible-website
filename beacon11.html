🎯 What you'll learn

In this Beacon, you'll create something personal.

A profile picture. A cover image. A poster. Something made for you, by you — with AI.

You'll use Leonardo AI to generate the image, and Canva AI to put the finishing touches on it.

🛠️ Meet the tool

You may have heard of Canva — it's one of the most popular design tools in the world. And it now has AI built right in.

👉 https://canva.com

Sign in with Google — your Gmail account works instantly.

Canva lets you take any image and turn it into a finished design. You can add text, change backgrounds, resize for any platform, and use AI to edit or enhance the image — all without any design experience.

Think of Leonardo AI as the artist, and Canva as the studio where the final piece comes together.

🖼 ️ What makes a great profile picture?

It doesn't have to be a photo of your face.

It can be an illustrated avatar, a stylised portrait, or something abstract that just feels like you.

The key is to describe a mood, a style, and a setting — not just "a person."

The more specific your prompt, the more personal the result.

🧪 Try it now

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

Step 1 — Generate your image in Leonardo AI

Go to 👉 https://leonardo.ai → sign in with Google → paste this prompt:

A warm illustrated portrait of a person sitting by a window, golden afternoon light, soft painterly style, minimal background, calm and thoughtful mood

Click Generate.

Not feeling it? Swap one detail — the setting, the light, the style — and generate again. Try: "evening light" instead of "golden afternoon", or "watercolour style" instead of "painterly style." One word, completely different result.

Step 2 — Bring it into Canva

Go to 👉 https://canva.com → on the homepage, click the Social media icon below the search bar.

You'll see a "Create a design" page. Pick a format:

  • Instagram Post (4:5) — classic square/portrait post
  • Instagram Story — vertical format
  • Facebook Post — wider layout

Browse the templates → click one you like → it opens in the editor.

On the left sidebar, click Uploads → Upload files → select your Leonardo image → click on it to place it on the canvas.

Add your name, a short line of text — or keep it purely visual.

👀 What to notice

Look at the final result in Canva — the image you generated, placed in a real design format, with your name on it.

It started as a single sentence in Leonardo AI.

That's the workflow behind most AI-assisted creative work: generate a raw ingredient, then shape it into something finished. The two steps together are more powerful than either tool alone.

💡 Good to know

Canva's free plan includes everything you need for this Beacon — no Pro subscription required.

If you want to use your AI-generated image as an actual profile picture, download it at the highest quality available. In Canva, click Share → Download → PNG.

Leonardo AI images are public by default on the free plan. If you'd prefer privacy, generate something stylised or abstract rather than anything personal.

Posting to Instagram — Once you're happy with your design, download it as a PNG or JPG (click Share → Download) and upload it to Instagram as you normally would. Canva doesn't post for you automatically on the free plan.
