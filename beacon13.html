🎯 What you'll learn

By the end of this Beacon, you'll know how to transform a photo using Canva's free

editing tools — no design skills needed. You'll apply filters, adjust light and color,

add text, and crop like a pro. The photo doesn't have to be perfect to start.

That's exactly the point.

🖼 ️ Your photo, AI's hands

Editing used to mean expensive software and hours of practice. Canva does the heavy

lifting for you. You move a slider. It updates instantly. That's it.

✨ Filters — one click, whole new mood

Open any photo in Canva's Photo editor. Click Filters and browse the presets.

One click changes the entire feeling of a photo — warmer, cooler, faded, dramatic.

Try at least five. You'll be surprised how different the same photo can look.

🎨 Adjust the look

Want your photo to feel warmer? Brighter? A little more dramatic?

Click Adjust to change brightness, contrast, saturation, shadows, and more —

with simple sliders.

No photography experience needed. Just move until it looks right to you.

✂ ️ Crop and straighten

Sometimes less is more. Cropping removes distractions and puts the focus exactly

where you want it.

Click Crop → drag the edges → hit Done.

Try the rule of thirds: place your subject slightly off-centre. It almost always

looks better than dead centre.

🔤 Add text to your photo

A single line of text can turn a photo into a statement.

Click Text in the left sidebar → choose a style → type something short.

Try: a location, a date, a mood, or just one word that fits the image.

🧪 Try it now

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

You'll need a photo for this one. Here are two easy options:

Option A — Use your own photo

A selfie, a pet, a landscape — anything works.

Option B — Grab a free stock photo

Go to 👉 https://unsplash.com and search for any of these:

  1. Coffee cup on a table
  2. Dog in a park
  3. Person smiling outdoors
  4. Flowers in a vase
  5. City street with buildings
  6. Cat sitting by a window
  7. Fruit on a white background
  8. Hands holding a book
  9. Bicycle leaning against a wall
  10. Sunset over water

Download one, then go to 👉 https://canva.com → click Photo editor → upload your photo.

Now try this — 3 steps:

Step 1 → Click Filters → try at least 5 different ones

Step 2 → Click Adjust → move the Brightness and Contrast sliders until it feels right

Step 3 → Click Crop → reframe the photo so the subject fills more of the image

Look at the result. Then look at where you started.

👀 What to notice

After applying a filter, look at how the mood of the photo changes — not just the

colors. A warm filter feels inviting. A cool one feels distant. A faded one feels

nostalgic. You didn't change the photo. You changed how it feels. That's the real

power of editing.

💡 Good to know

All the tools in this Beacon — Filters, Adjust, Crop, and Text — are completely

free on Canva's free plan. No subscription needed.

Nothing is permanent until you download. You can undo anything. Experiment freely.

If you want to try AI-powered tools like Background Remover or Magic Eraser,

those are available on Canva Pro — worth exploring if you use Canva regularly.
