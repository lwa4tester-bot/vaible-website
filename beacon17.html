🎯 What you'll learn

In this Beacon, you'll watch AI turn spoken words into text — automatically.

No typing. No transcribing. Just press a button and let CapCut listen.

You'll also try translating those captions into another language — in one click.

🛠️ Meet the tool

CapCut is a free video editing tool — simple enough for beginners, powerful enough for professionals.

Its AI features are genuinely impressive. And you can use it right in your browser.

👉 Open CapCut here → https://www.capcut.com

No download needed. A free account is all you need to get started.

🎤 Why captions matter

Captions make videos easier to follow — even without sound.

They help people who speak a different language. They make your content accessible to more people.

Adding them used to take hours of careful work.

Now AI does it in seconds.

✂ ️ How it works

CapCut's AI listens to the audio in your video.

It writes out the words — and times them, so each caption appears at exactly the right moment.

Then, if you want, it translates everything into another language. Automatically.

You don't need to know the language. You don't need to type a single word.

🧪 Try it now

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

1
Open CapCut and go to AI captions
  1. Go to https://www.capcut.com and click Try online for free
  2. Sign in with your Google account
  3. On the home screen, look for the "You may want to try" row
  4. Click AI captions
2
Choose your video

You have two options — pick whichever feels easier:

📱 Use your own video

Click Upload video and choose a clip from your device.

Any video under 20 minutes works. A short one (under 2 minutes) is perfect for this.

🎬 Use a sample clip

Scroll down to "No videos? Try our sample clips"

Click Learn foreign language — CapCut loads it automatically.

3
Translate the captions

Look at the bottom left corner — find the xA icon in the Shortcuts row.

Click it, pick a language, and see the translation appear instantly. 🌍

💡 Good to know

CapCut works best with clear audio. Background noise can confuse it — but it still does remarkably well.

If a caption isn't quite right, you can click on it and fix it manually. It only takes a second.

The free version includes everything you need for this Beacon. No payment required.

CapCut is also available as a mobile app — so you can do all of this from your phone if you prefer.

✅ Beacon complete

You just added captions and translated a video — without touching a single subtitle file.

That's a real skill. Content creators pay professionals to do exactly this.
