Research Like
a Pro

You'll use Perplexity and NotebookLM to research smarter, not harder.

07
🎯 What you'll learn

By the end of this Beacon, you'll know how to search the web with sources you can check — and how to ask questions about your own documents. Two new tools: Perplexity and NotebookLM.

🛠️ Meet the tools

Perplexity — your smart search engine

Perplexity is an AI-powered search engine. Instead of a list of links, you get a direct answer — with sources included. → perplexity.ai

Free tier: Unlimited basic searches. Pro Searches (advanced queries) capped at 5 per day.

NotebookLM — your reading assistant

NotebookLM is a Google tool that lets you upload your own documents — and then ask questions about them. → notebooklm.google.com

Free tier: 100 notebooks, 50 sources per notebook, 50 chat queries per day, 3 Audio Overviews per day.
🔍 Why not just ask a chatbot?

Research needs sources

ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini are great at writing, explaining, and brainstorming. But research needs one extra thing: you want to see where the information comes from.

That's exactly what these two tools add:

  • Perplexity searches the live internet and attaches sources to every answer — you can click and check each claim.
  • NotebookLM doesn't search the internet at all. It reads only the documents you give it, so the answers stay grounded in your own material.

Same question, two very different approaches. You'll feel the difference in a minute.

🧪 Try it now

Two tools, one topic

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

Step 1 — Perplexity

Ready-made prompt
"What are the pros and cons of drinking coffee every day?"

Step 2 — NotebookLM

Go to notebooklm.google.com, create a new notebook, add en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee as a website source, then ask:

NotebookLM prompt
"What are the main health effects of coffee? Give me the key points in simple language."
👀 What to notice

Two different superpowers

With Perplexity: the answer comes with sources attached — you can see exactly where each claim comes from. With NotebookLM: it only used your article. No guessing, no internet search — just what you gave it. Two different tools, two different superpowers.

💡 Good to know

They make a great team

Here's a trick for bigger research projects: use the two tools in sequence.

First, ask Perplexity to find good articles on your topic. Then copy the best source links into a NotebookLM notebook — and now you can ask detailed questions about all of them in one place.

Search wide with Perplexity, dig deep with NotebookLM. 😊
