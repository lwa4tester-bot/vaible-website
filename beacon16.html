Music
From Nothing

You'll type a short description of a mood — and two AI tools will turn it into a full song.

16
🎯 What you'll learn

You've heard AI can write, draw, and even speak in someone else's voice.

Now it's going to compose music for you.

In this Beacon, you'll type a short description of a mood, a scene, or a feeling — and two AI tools will turn it into a full song. Vocals. Instruments. Melody. All from a few words.

No musical talent required.

🛠️ Meet the tools

Suno → https://suno.com

One of the most popular AI music generators out there. Type a prompt, press Create, and within seconds you have a complete song — lyrics, melody, vocals and all. Fast, surprisingly polished, and free to try.

Sonauto → https://sonauto.ai

A slightly different take on the same idea. Sonauto lets you steer the mood, genre, and energy in its own way. Same concept — words in, music out — but the result often has a very different feel.

🎼 Prompting music is different

When you chat with Claude or ChatGPT, you ask questions.

With music generators, you paint a picture.

You're not asking — you're describing. A mood, a moment, a sound.

This works:"Upbeat acoustic song about a Sunday morning, happy, soft guitar, sunshine"

This doesn't get very far:"Make me a song."

The more specific you are about the feeling, the more the result will surprise you in a good way.

🧪 Try it now

Screens and menus may look slightly different — that's normal. Just explore and you'll find your way. 😊

Use the exact same prompt in both tools — then listen to what comes back.

Your prompt: "Gentle acoustic song about a rainy afternoon, calm and cozy, soft guitar, a little nostalgic"

Step 1 — Suno → https://suno.com

Set the version to v4.5-all at the top. Turn Instrumental off if you want vocals. Paste the prompt and click Create. You'll get 4 versions — some may be v5.5 Preview (1 minute only). To download: ... → Download → Download Anyway

Step 2 — Sonauto → https://sonauto.ai

Have your phone nearby — you may need two-factor authentication. Paste the same prompt and click Generate. You'll get 2 versions. Wait until fully loaded, then download: ... → Download

Want to try a different mood instead? Pick one from the list and build your own prompt around it:

  1. A road trip song with the windows down
  2. A motivational gym anthem
  3. A lullaby for a restless night
  4. A jazz café on a Friday evening
  5. A heroic moment from an imaginary film
  6. A cheerful birthday song
  7. A quiet, melancholy song about missing someone
👀 What to notice

Listen to both songs without judgment. Then ask yourself: which one felt closer to what you had in your head?

The same sentence produced two completely different songs. That's not a flaw — that's each AI interpreting your words through its own creative lens.

The aha: Your prompt wasn't a command. It was an invitation. And each tool accepted it differently.

💡 Good to know

Suno collects your prompts and may use them to train their AI models. Avoid typing anything personal — stick to moods, genres, and fictional scenes.

AI-generated music sits in a legal grey area — copyright rules vary by platform and country. For personal enjoyment, learning, or sharing with friends, you're absolutely fine. Downloading is free via Download Anyway — but commercial use requires a paid plan.

Suno's free plan gives you 50 credits that refresh daily. Sonauto also offers free generations. No payment needed to complete this Beacon.

And yes — the lyrics the AI writes are often wonderfully strange. That's part of the fun. 😄

No rush. Come back whenever you're ready. 🙂
